Since the time species became self-cognisant, homo-sapiens apart from ensuring survival needs, also look for satiating other biological needs. Which attributes contribute to the notion of prostitution being one of the oldest professions of the civilised world?

After some time, the curiosity of metaphysical essence took over. This species pondered about the reason for existence, the virtues of good or bad humans, the existence post death, a superior being who created it all, etc. But somewhere down the line, humans needed validation. External validation can be about anything: being right, being desirable, being trampled on, or just mere existence. No matter how well the internal understanding of things was, isolation wasn’t cutting it. It required a stamp of outer approval from time to time.

The sense of possession, intermingled with psychological and spiritual needs that are stated above, and the “duty” of procreation, might have given birth to companionship. Through different times and cultures, this concept was different. In some, a man having multiple partners, a woman.. in some, both; “open marriages” as we know it today. Most prevalent was a man having multiple committed paramours, which is why patriarchal societies are more common. Some are and were matriarchal.

However, as civilisation went ahead with tracing the dimensions of time and innovation with it, new professions came, and societal standings changed. Things that were uncontestedly the best things to do became contested. With this homogenisation also came equality and feminism. Plato famously said, “A woman can be a just ruler, if educated like a man”. With this came the wind of the “one man — one woman” philosophy.

Many cultures and religions prohibited separation, while some allowed it. Some understood human needs and allowed remarriage if a partner dies; in some religions, men and women both, in some, either one. With the ravages of time came the cultural digression; growing ease of human life rendered more legroom for experimentation and exploration. Humans became aware of their needs, which they’re agnostic about. Beyond normalcy of satisfaction, dedication, and all, masochism, sadism, guilt-driven lust, self-denigrating lust, promiscuity, curiosity of diversified physical indulgence, etc.

Debauchery became commonplace, and people became more and more faithless. Then came academicians who advocated for different things. Some said, COMPANIONSHIP IS OR LIMITED TO SPIRITUAL, EMOTIONAL, AND COGNITIVE NEEDS, whereas COITUS IS STRICTLY A BIOLOGICAL NEED. It shouldn’t be confused, which is why “past is past”, “person before and person now, “exploring is fine”, and “satisfying the bodily needs is important for psychology “ are popular.

In the orthodox sense, however, academicians said, “COITUS IS, AND SHOULD BE A WAY OF EXPRESSING THOSE EMOTIONAL AND CONGNITIVE INTIMACIES”, whereas, “IT IS NOT STRICTLY BIOLOGICAL AND IT IS EMBEDDED IN BASIC HUMAN PSYCHO, ABANDONING IT MAKES HUMANS NO DIFFERENT THAN UNINTELLIGENT ANIMALS.”

Some other academicians said, “IT’S BOTH OF THE ABOVE, AND IT’S TIME THAT DECIDES WHICH IS WHICH ONE FOR A COURSE OF HUMAN LIFE.” There are social issues like crimes, belittlement of people with no choices, ostracisation of night workers, and furthermore. What is right and wrong is heavily determined by the cultural and social influences mingled with instilled values during upbringing.
