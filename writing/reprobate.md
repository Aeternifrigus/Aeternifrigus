Destiny, fate, randomness, or anything at all. What do we or we should call the elements of our lives that we can't control? Sometimes it feels like someone is conscious and watching and making them play, sometimes it feels just random. Whatever happens to us is not because of us.

We work with the belief in something. Can be the promised utopia of heaven, or simply for the sake of something we believe is tantamount to existence, or humanity, or something selfish. That belief stems from the fact that whether we believe the sea of life we are sailing at is having waves that are natural or intentional. Some say it's intentional. Some say it's random. Each has their own faith.

Let's assume that there is indeed something divine that's making things at play. Do we blame it for the atrocities of mankind on mankind itself? Do we blame the moral bankruptcy and the inhumane nature of the current world on it? A bit rich! Some would say. Morals, ethics, and values are something we made. We had the freedom of doing whatever we could, considering the nature of nature that we can't fully control. Yet, can we blame god for whatever we do to our kin and planet with our selfishness? No, right? We have had the freedom to do whatever. After all, we are the most powerful species on this planet.

But then it poses the question, why? Why do we exist? What purpose do we serve? Is god some kind of entity that needs power and divinity from our prayers to become powerful? Is that why we were created? Just like one builds a poultry farm for eggs and chickens? Is that the case? All the ups and downs we grow through, all the struggles and reactions to them, the prayers we make. Is that why we were created? But, if divinity from our prayers is indeed some sort of energy, then what about energy being neither created nor destroyed? Hey, but there is a balance. There always has been.

More people mean fewer trees, and that means less oxygen. Less oxygen translates to poor growth. Our brain, organs, and everything need oxygen to expand and be better. The reason people used to live longer in the past if they didn't get any sickness. Organic. Natural. So we may be high in numbers, but we settled for a reduced standard of living to accommodate our growing numbers. When resources are scarce, wars happen. Taking down the numbers and rebalancing. The game of energies and nature.

With our higher numbers, higher intensity of our emotions, and prayers, the divinity is converted at the price of fewer natural resources. If the god is omnipotent, did it have a reason to create us in order to convert energies into divinity? So if god is omnipotent and omnipresent, then this logic doesn't hold true. God could convert without us. God is then just energy incarnate, something present everywhere, at the essence of everything, making everything possible, making existence possible, and at the root of the creation of this universe. So the question is whether it does indeed have a consciousness or not.. but if it isn't omnipotent, omniscient, and omnipresent, former logic is true, where we are all cattle of the god.

Now coming down to the thread, where god is indeed omnipotent, the question hangs on whether it is conscious or not. Let's assume god is conscious, with personality, will, and all. Then why do we exist? For its mere entertainment? Really? Then why are we promised salvation if we pray? Or is it just something mankind made up to believe in life? So either we are mere entertainment, or we came into being due to nature, and god had no hand nor intention behind it. If we are mere entertainment, you know what to do, but I can't live with the idea that I am mere entertainment.

Hence, god is conscious, where our creation is unintentional and a mere flow of nature, or god is not conscious, in which case god = energy. Both cases lead to one conclusion, world and our lives are random. Nothing is happening consciously in someone's direction. It is just our intelligence and logic making randomness seem like a puzzle. We make sense out of it, not that it had any preconceived sense of happening. If we go by the religious understanding of a "god," then this belief renders that we have been abandoned. We are mere REPROBATES, living for our own, for the sake of our own, and reasons of our own making if there's any.
