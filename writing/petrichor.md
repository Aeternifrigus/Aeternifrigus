Rain is that sign of life for me. As if nature hasn’t abandoned us. It still looks over us. Calming the soul, barren land; giving life to something devoid of it. Enriching that dusty, dead essence by adding character to it.

I see trees expressing their joy through their leaves, in anticipation of rain. Organisms celebrate the upcoming downpour of nectar of vital spirit. Invigorating, delighting, just like the healing spell cast by a mage. Washing away the contaminations, both external and internal. Hiding the pain of tears in its chill yet warm shroud of water droplets.

Thunder rumbling in the sky, surrounded by those dark, beautiful clouds, with a hint of sunlight. Just as if, hiding us, embracing us, from that ever-so-painful sunlight, which is an eternal reminder of our mortality through its cycle of revolution. Telling us, it’s fine. Thunder, telling us so; so blissful, but harsh if required. Punishing yet not losing its divine charm; fascinating us, even when it’s cruel.

Snowing, in that chilly winter, taking away some pain of that harsh, dry bone piercing gelid reality. As a layer of defence in the form of moisture, as a form of rekindler of vitality. Answering the cries of those soul-dried tree barks. In all of it, telling, even if the sun and moon have betrayed, she is there. There for us, us puny satires of the universe, destined to entertain through our cycle of transduction, god knows who. Perhaps even god is mere entertainment to it, given that it exists.

Thunder, water or ice, clouds, space, it has it all, except soil. Earth. When it includes that also in this beautiful synergy of elements, what we get is PETRICHOR. The pre-sign of blessing that’s supposed to come. The undeniable evidence that blessing has descended and also the reminder of blessings’ existence after it’s completed. The most fragrant, mesmerising, alive odour. Representing that unison of the 5.

Today, like every time, I will get drenched whilst smoking. Assimilating, embracing this awe-inspiring phenomenon of nature by being synchronous with its flow of energies. Just like my chlorophyll friends, giving that slight brownish tint to the leaves before it rains, before they become lush green again. Showing the sign of their death before getting enriched. As I once told my brother Mota, life is all about the contrast. It’s the contrast. Chlorophylls get it. And my, do those beautiful orange-green leaves look beautiful against that backdrop of grey?

With that thought, a very fine greeting. Petrichor will bless you, if it hasn’t today, when it’s due.
